One-day hands-on workshop

Engineering Sustainable Agentic AI Systems

Can we engineer Agentic AI systems that are not just intelligent, but also sustainable?

SustAInd (Sustainable AI for India) is organising this in-person workshop at the Software Engineering Research Center (SERC), IIIT Hyderabad.

Register your interestFree. 30 seats. Register by 30 November.

About the workshop

Agentic AI systems call large language models and tools in a loop until a task is done. A single request can turn into many model calls, so energy use and cost grow in ways that are hard to see from the outside. Each extra component and prompt also adds to what the team has to maintain later.

Much of this footprint is decided early. The choice of model and the way an agent is broken into steps shape it at design time. Once the system runs, it can adapt to keep the footprint in check as load and conditions change. This workshop treats sustainability as an engineering concern at both stages.

We start with the basics of AI agents and an introduction to SustAInd. The hands-on sessions then cover the topics below, and the day ends with a team hackathon where each group works on one problem statement.

  • Measuring energy consumption
  • Optimizing agent designs
  • Assessing technical debt
  • Exploring runtime adaptations

What you will learn

  • Explain, in plain terms, what an agentic AI system is.
  • Describe sustainability as more than "green". It spans environmental, economic, technical, social and individual dimensions.
  • See why agentic systems raise new sustainability concerns.
  • Recognize that architecture decisions shape sustainability.
  • Get a feel for SustAInd's research and apply one idea in a small group exercise.
